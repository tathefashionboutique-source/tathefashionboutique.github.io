HOW TO ADD PHOTOS
1. Open the images folder.
2. Copy your own photos into it and rename each photo using the names above.
3. Upload index.html, style.css, script.js, and the images folder together to your hosting provider.
4. Refresh the live website.

The six category cards use online sample cover images. The individual style slots are prepared for your own photos. Until you add local photos, slots display the STYLE name and “ADD YOUR PHOTO”.
